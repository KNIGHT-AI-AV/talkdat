# Live Captions

Open **Live Captions** from Tools or the Pill menu. Press **Start** to show your
speech in a floating caption strip. It uses your downloaded local speech model
and the microphone selected in Settings.

The button shows **Preparing** while the local model loads, with the microphone
off. If a model is missing, prepare it in Settings first. **Starting** means the
microphone is opening; **Stop** appears once it is listening. Closing the strip,
Panic Stop or quitting also stops this capture. **Stopping** remains visible
while its work retires, so repeated clicks do not create overlapping streams.

The strip follows the microphone's actual audio format, including a device that
opens at 44.1 or 48 kHz. A failed microphone, interrupted audio or a model that
cannot keep up produces an error you can read and retry. The app does not keep
an unlimited backlog of delayed captions. Stop discards pending caption audio
and prevents late words from appearing.

Use the font controls, drag/resize and Pager control to adjust the display.
The Glass/Clear control changes its presentation where the operating system
supports it. The displayed text is a short live tail, not a saved transcript;
use Ramble or normal dictation when you need to keep the words.

This strip is separate from the optional words shown on the Pill during normal
dictation, described in [Local live captions](LIVE_CAPTIONS_LOCAL.md).
