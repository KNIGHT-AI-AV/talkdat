# Your saved activity

Open **Tools > Stats** for saved word counts, today's entries, your current
streak and the past seven calendar days. **Read History** opens the saved text.
**Refresh** reads the latest saved entries while the window stays usable. If
reading fails, the last successful figures remain available and you can retry.

Saved words include dictations, translations and rewrites. Estimated speech time
and typing time saved use only dictation entries. Typing time compares 40 typed
words per minute with 150 spoken words per minute; these are assumptions,
not a measurement of your speed. Speech time is estimated from words, not
recorded audio duration. Languages without spaces may be undercounted.

Dates use this computer's time zone. The streak counts consecutive days ending
today. An entry with an unreadable date still contributes its text to totals,
but not to the calendar. Stats explains when dates are missing or the history
exceeds the most recent 20,000 entries. If saving history is off, earlier saved
entries can still appear; unsaved dictations are not included.

The current speech provider and model describe your current settings. Earlier
entries may have used other routes. Open **About these estimates** for the
assumptions behind any processing projection. The rate table is dated August 4,
2026 and may be outdated. A projection assumes your average dictation-day volume
on all 30 days; it excludes Talk DAT plans, writing models and other charges.
It is not an invoice. A local speech route having no per-minute provider charge
does not describe the price of your Talk DAT plan.

Stats reads aggregate activity. It does not change your saved entries or send
their text into the Stats page.
