# Personal words in local dictation

Add unusual names and spellings in Words & Phrases. Supported local speech
models can use those entries while recognizing speech, before the usual
dictionary and formatting pass. Hints are a preference, so they cannot
guarantee a spelling. Numbers, links and file paths are excluded from recognition hints.
The preference remains small until more of a taught spelling is heard.

After a successful Fix That spelling correction, Talk DAT can offer to
remember the spelling and what it misheard. Accept the offer to save it;
dismissing it leaves your dictionary unchanged. Broader rewrites and copied
or failed edits do not create this offer. Turn word learning off in
Words & Phrases to disable it. Existing curated entries take precedence.

The local model remains your choice. The September 2026 comparison found
that the tested Voxtral model reduced errors modestly on public read speech
but took longer to finish than Parakeet on the test computer. That result
does not justify changing your selected model or downloading a new one.
