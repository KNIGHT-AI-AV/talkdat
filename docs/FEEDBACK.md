# Share an idea or request a language

Open **Help > Share an idea** or **Help > Request a language**. The two forms
keep separate drafts while you move between pages or close and reopen the
settings window. These drafts stay in memory and clear when you quit Talk DAT.

Describe what happens now and what you would like to happen. For a language
request, include the language and region. A reply email is optional. The
2,000-character limit includes the title or language name; some emoji count
as more than one character. A longer draft remains available to edit or copy
in full, but cannot be sent until it fits.

Choose **Send to the team** when ready. You can visit another page while it
sends and return to the result. Talk DAT shows **Received** only after its
service confirms receipt. An unconfirmed attempt keeps the message available
to copy or retry. There is no automatic retry; an unconfirmed attempt may
already have reached the service if its reply was interrupted.

Your message, app version, platform, plan and optional reply address are sent.
Formatting logs are left out by default. If you choose **Preview formatting
log**, review the exact excerpt shown, which can contain dictated text before
and after formatting. **Attach this excerpt** selects that text for your next
send. Cancelling the preview leaves it out. Each send requires a new choice,
and an older preview may need refreshing. Previewing does not submit anything.

**Copy message** copies your complete title or language request and details,
without the formatting log. **Open email draft** requests a draft in your mail
app. You must finish sending there; opening a draft does not confirm delivery
and never attaches formatting logs.

If the shared interface cannot start, the existing native form remains
available. It also requires a service receipt and offers email as a separate
action. Its existing log checkbox is optional; the exact-excerpt preview
belongs to the shared interface.
